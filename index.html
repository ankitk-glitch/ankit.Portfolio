About Me

Engineering Systems,
Operations & Business Strategy

I am a final-year Civil Engineering undergraduate student at Delhi Technological University (DTU) operating at the intersection of Product Management, Technical Operations, Data Analytics, and Business Strategy. Combining quantitative engineering problem-solving (JEE Main Rank ~85K, Advanced ~22.5K) with military-grade leadership, I specialize in transforming unstructured operational problems into structured, data-informed systems.

My leadership footprint spans high-stakes environments—serving as Junior Under Officer (JUO) in the NCC commanding 250+ cadets, directing 10-day CATC training camps, and managing logistics for SAAMANT, an inter-college festival engaging 3,000+ participants and a ₹2.5 Lakh budget. Simultaneously, I have applied engineering precision to global digital workflows, delivering 25+ BIM models with Celekhor GmbH (Germany) and managing operations at Studio Orange.

As an entrepreneur and product thinker, I co-founded Ribhus (a BIM and digital engineering studio) and engineered Cadet OS—an end-to-end attendance and duty allocation product system complete with full PRD specifications. My commitment to impact is highlighted by conceptualizing the Affordable EMG Bionic Arm, which secured 1st Place at the Delhi Directorate Idea & Innovation Competition and qualified as an All-India Finalist in Vijayawada.

Fluent in SQL, Power BI, Advanced Excel, Python for data analytics, AutoCAD, ArchiCAD, and product frameworks (PRD writing, user journey mapping, KPI design, A/B testing), I am dedicated to driving operational excellence, product clarity, and scalable growth across technology and business organizations.
